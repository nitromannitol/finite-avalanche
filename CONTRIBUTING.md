# Contributing

Use the pinned Lean, Mathlib and Lattice-Probability versions. Run the build and axiom checks in the README. Use a fresh checkout for release verification and preserve established dependency checkouts.

Keep the manuscript's carriers, quantifiers, shared constants, copied-edge multiplicities and electrical normalization. Prove existence and uniqueness before choosing a characterized object. A supporting lemma does not certify a numbered paper statement.

Production files must have no incomplete proof or undeclared axiom. Fix Lean warnings rather than suppress them. The allowed boundary is the three logical axioms and the exact registered external exports. A replacement for an external result needs an exact statement comparison, an actual proof and axiom check, and an independent audit before the registry changes.

Put reusable probability and network results in Lattice-Probability. Copy or adapt useful source from other developments with its attribution and required license notices; do not add those developments as dependencies without an explicit project decision.

The comparator challenge contains one intentional theorem hole and stays outside production targets. Preserve the sandbox, pinned tools, complete theorem statement and required Lean and NanoDa checks. Keep development transcripts and historical audit archives outside the public source tree.
